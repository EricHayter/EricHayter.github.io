Things I'm into:

  • Databases. They're some of the most vital pieces of software, used everywhere in and outside of tech. With the amount of data in the world growing, how can we build systems that scale to modern-day workloads?
  • Systems programming. A lot of the world's most critical software (networking infrastructure, banking, etc.) is still written in systems languages. How can we write code that is both safe and performant, while staying maintainable?
  • AI inference. AI is the greatest invention of my (limited) lifetime, but cost and availability still limit who gets to use it. With demand only growing (bigger models, longer context windows, more agents), how can we serve models efficiently enough to make them widely available?
